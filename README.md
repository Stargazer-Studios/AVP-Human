# AVP (Human)

Welcome to AVP (Human)! This Minecraft mod is made in 1.21.1 and is home to human-related content from the Alien, Predator and AVP franchises. The mod is currently available for Fabric and NeoForge.

---

## MIGRATING FROM "AVP"

If you are migrating from the 1.21.1 "AVP" mod (found here: https://modrinth.com/mod/avp) to the split up AVP files, you MUST use ALL modules provided (Alien, Human and Predator) to avoid losing data in your worlds and/or corrupting your worlds. Do not use the standalone AVP mod as it will no longer be updated.

Also, please **BACK UP YOUR WORLDS** before migrating! The different modules should migrate their respective content automatically, but it never hurts to back things up just in case! :)

## Features

### Colonial Marines

Recruit and command intelligent marine NPCs with advanced Goal-Oriented Action Planning (GOAP) AI:

- **Full Inventory System** - 27-slot inventory for weapons, armor, and supplies.
- **Squad System** - Assign squad leaders and coordinate marine groups.
- **Dynamic Equipment** - Marines automatically evaluate and equip the best available weapons and armor.
- **Combat AI** - Engage hostiles with tactical positioning and weapon selection.
- **Survival Behaviors** - Self-healing, fire resistance management, fall damage prevention, and more.

Marines spawn naturally in military structures and as patrols throughout your world or can be recruited to defend your base.

### Weapons

Arm yourself with **11 unique firearms** and **1 throwable**, each with distinct handling characteristics:

- **M41A Pulse Rifle** - The iconic 4-round burst rifle from the films. 99-round capacity.
- **F903WE Rifle** - Reliable semi-automatic rifle. 32-round capacity.
- **M4RA Battle Rifle** - Precision marksman rifle. 90-round capacity.
- **M42A3 Sniper Rifle** - Long-range rifle with 128-block effective range.
- **M56 Smartgun** - High-velocity smartgun with 500-round drum.
- **Old Painless** - Heavy rotating minigun for sustained fire.
- **M88 Mod4 Combat Pistol** - Reliable sidearm. 18-round magazine.
- **M37/12 Shotgun** - Close-quarters devastation.
- **ZX-76 Shotgun** - Alternative pump-action shotgun.
- **M6B Rocket Launcher** - Explosive ordnance launcher.
- **Flamethrower Sevastopol** - Area-denial flame weapon.
- **Grenades** - Standard explosive, incendiary, and irradiated variants for tactical options.

### Armor Sets

Protect yourself with **8 complete armor sets**, each with 4 pieces (helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots):

- **Steel Armor** - Durable armor that is a tier above iron.
- **Titanium Armor** - Superior protection with the highest durability, offering a nice middle ground between iron and diamonds.
- **Tactical Armor** - Military-grade combat armor that offers bullet resistance.
- **Tactical Camo Armor** - Camouflage variant of the tactical armor.
- **Pressure Suit** - Environmental protection for hazardous conditions. Allows you to permanently breathe underwater!
- **WY Commando Armor** - Advanced corporate military gear. Wearing the helmet gives you night vision!
- **WY Elite Armor** - Top-tier Weyland-Yutani equipment
- **MK50 Armor** - Specialized armor that protects you from radiation.
- **APE Armor** - All-around defensive armor that protects you from fire, cold and even facehuggers from avp: alien or gigeresque!

### Defensive Blocks

**Sentry Turret** - Deploy automated defensive turrets with:
- Configurable field of view and targeting range.
- Integration with ammo chests for automatic reloading.
- Adjustable damage output.

**Razor Wire** - Defensive barriers that damage entities on contact

### Building Materials

Over **150 building blocks** across multiple material types, each with full stair and slab variants:

**Steel Blocks**
- Steel plating, grating, fastened siding, and tread patterns.
- Doors, trapdoors, bars, chain fences, and columns.
- Buttons and pressure plates.

**Titanium Blocks**
- Complete parallel set to steel with titanium variants.
- Superior aesthetics for high-tech installations.

**Ferroaluminum Blocks**
- Full suite of industrial building materials.

**Industrial Blocks**
- Industrial concrete and glass variants.
- Padding blocks for interior spaces.
- Plastic blocks for utilitarian construction.

**Colored Concrete**
- Stairs and slabs for all 16 dye colors.

### Radiation System

A dynamic radiation mechanic with three phases:

1. **Incubation (0-20%)** - No damage as the body adjusts.
2. **Peak Damage (20-80%)** - Maximum radiation sickness with escalating side effects.
3. **Taper-off (80-100%)** - Symptoms subside as the effect wears off.

Side effects progress from weakness and hunger to slowness and blindness at higher exposure levels. Radiation-resistant armor provides protection.

**Radioactive Materials:**
- Autunite ore and blocks emit radiation.
- Irradiated grenades spread contamination.
- Lead chests provide shielded storage for radioactive items.

### Gene System

A genetics system allowing modification of entity attributes:

- **Gene Reader** - Analyze genetic traits on entities.
- **Syringe** - Apply genetic modifications.
- Multiple gene types affecting attributes, applying effects, or serving as markers.

### Resources & Crafting

**New Ores:**
- Titanium Ore (including deepslate variant).
- Bauxite Ore (aluminum source).
- Galena Ore (lead source).
- Monazite Ore.
- Autunite Ore (radioactive).
- Lithium Ore (reactive).
- Zinc Ore (including deepslate variant).

**Processed Materials:**
- Steel, Titanium, Lead, Brass, Aluminum, Uranium, Zinc, and Ferroaluminum ingots.
- Carbon dust, lithium dust, and silicon.

**Electronics:**
- CPUs, diodes, integrated circuits, LEDs, resistors, transistors, capacitors, servos, and more!

**Weapon Components:**
- Barrels, grips, stocks, receivers, and specialized parts for crafting firearms.

**Tools:**
- Steel and Titanium tool sets (pickaxe, axe, hoe, shovel, sword).

### Structures

Military installations spawn naturally throughout your world:

- **Marine Camp** - Squad encampments with marines and supplies.
- **Supply Outpost** - Resource depots (grassland, desert, and badlands variants).
- **Munitions Outpost** - Weapons and ammunition production.
- **Communications Outpost** - Signal relay and command centers.
- **Mobile Lab** - Portable research facilities.

Each structure contains loot chests, marine patrols, and defensive positions.

### Utility Items

- **Ammo Chest** - Specialized storage that feeds ammunition to turrets and marines.
- **Lead Chest** - Radiation-shielded storage for hazardous materials.
- **Industrial Furnace** - Faster smelting for building block recipes.
- **Armor Case** - Compact armor storage. Stores a full set in a single slot!
- **Canisters** - Transport water, lava, milk, and powder snow with increased storage, 8x that of a bucket!
- **Dog Tags** - Identity items for marines to remember the fallen.
- **Gun Blueprints** - Collectible crafting components for all weapons.

### Villager Integration

**Commissary** - A new villager profession with 5 trading levels offering military supplies, ammunition, and equipment.
