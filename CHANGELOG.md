# v0.1.7

## ✍️ Developer Notes
- For help or other questions, concerns, etc. check out our Discord server: https://discord.gg/wp7mvmbkVb

## ✨ What's New
- Marines can now move to and pick up water buckets.
- Added tactical knife item.
  - Made from 1 steel ingot and 1 polymer.
  - Swings faster than a sword, but deals less damage.
- Marines of all types now spawn with tactical knives.
  - Useful for when marines run out of ammo.
  - This change does not apply retroactively to already-spawned marines.

## 🐞 Fixes
- Fixed razor wire not being pathfindable.
- Fixed marines being unable to open doors.
- Fixed marines getting stuck in razor wire blocks.
- Fixed marines sometimes moving away from their leader after they teleport to their leader.
- Fixed marine pathing not updating frequently (which led to the marines zigzagging or not following the player optimally).
- Fixed marines potentially changing wandering position target if their original path becomes invalid.
- Fixed gigeresque acid breaking industrial glass and plastic.
